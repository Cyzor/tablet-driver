MockTab 0.4.0 improves pen feel, adds panning as a button action, and provides more Xencelabs Pen Display controls. It also improves the reliability of undo, backup, and settings migration.  This releases recognizes more devices, though new entries still need confirmation.

## Pen feel and panning

- **Pan View** — a new button action that grabs and drags the canvas like a hand tool, with momentum after release. Panning locks to the dominant axis to avoid drift.  Not available in all applications and websites.
- **Relative cursor movement** feels smoother, with a toggle for binding to a button.
- **Pressure Smoothing** control in Pen Feel damps sensor noise.
- **Stabilization eases off during fast strokes** instead of lagging every stroke evenly.
- **Drag Threshold** control; Tip‑up Assist is now a slider.

## Buttons and modes

- **Pen and touch ring diagrams are clickable** — click the tip, eraser, or a button to record its binding.
- **Touch‑ring and dial modes appear as a readable list**, with an editor that expands in place.
- **Touch‑ring scroll direction now matches the touch strip** on every hardware family.
- **Onboard bezel buttons get their own Buttons‑pane section.**
- **Reset any pane to its defaults** from a new command.
- Per‑app overrides now cover the Touch pane.

## Tell identical devices apart

- **Each physical tablet gets its own settings and profiles** — even two units of the same model. A second identical tablet no longer inherits or overwrites the first one's setup.
- **Quick Keys stays editable while the puck is disconnected.**

## Xencelabs Pen Display

- **Contrast, gamma, and color‑space controls** in the Displays pane.
- **Bezel‑button LED color control**; every device light shares one swatch and brightness editor.
- **Quick Keys battery level** in the Buttons pane.

## Reliability and data safety

- **Undo and redo now work correctly in more areas** — profile deletion, tool and tablet removal, and several other paths.
- **Restore app overrides, presets, and per‑tool settings from a backup.**
- **Settings survive app upgrades** — button bindings and pressure curves carry across versions.
- **Data collection captures more of what the tablet sends.** Analysis tools gather tablet information with better efficiency.
- **The Xencelabs wireless dongle no longer splits a puck into a second device.** A puck connected over the dongle now shares the settings of the wired one instead of starting an empty namespace beside it.
- Capture files from the data‑collection tool no longer record the device serial number.
- MockTab now tells Bluetooth tools apart by tool code, not serial alone; keyboard shortcuts on the remove alerts work again.

## Device support

- **Wacom Bamboo Pen & Touch (CTL‑460)** gains its own identity and decoding, but still needs verification.
- **Xencelabs Pen Display 16** joins the registry, but still needs verification.
- **A generic HID digitizer path** drives standard pen digitizers that have no dedicated decoder.
- **More legacy and Wacom tablets recognized**, drawn from the libwacom database.  Recognition is a prerequisite for proper support.
- Corrected identities for three early Bamboo models.

## Interface and fixes

- The pen‑display cursor offset is now a draggable area with direct interaction.
- Live pen coordinates no longer re‑render every settings pane, cutting idle CPU.
- The tilt visualizer no longer halts while the pen hovers; the pen display's mapped area stays coherent while disconnected.

## Internal

- New rolling snapshot build workflow.
- TabletKit decoder test suite: **348 tests, all passing** (up from 322).

## Compatibility

macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, universal binary — same as 0.3.0. Settings and profiles carry over with nothing to migrate.
