MockTab 0.4.1 adds a new pressure threshold setting and more reliable touch controls. It also includes device-recognition fixes and lower idle memory use.

Touch improvements, including pinch to zoom, scrolling refinements, and palm rejection, are contributions from @MarsLuay.  Thank you!

## Pen feel

- New **Click Threshold** setting controls how much pressure a tap needs to register as a click.

## Touch

- Optional two-finger **pinch to zoom**.
- Smoother, more predictable two-finger scrolling, touch-ring scrolling, and Xeneclabs dial motion.
- Palm rejection for IntuosV2 touch models.
- Improved panning stability at slow speeds.

## Fixes and reliability

- Fixed **Reset to Defaults**, View-menu shortcut syncing, and unavailable pane shortcuts.
- Window indicator when a tablet is no longer connected.
- Fixed conflicts between wired and wireless Xencelabs Quick Keys connections.

## Device support

- Improved recognition and corrected mappings for many Intuos, Bamboo, Graphire, Cintiq, CTL, and CTH models.
- Unrecognized tablets can now wake without restarting MockTab.

## Compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later; universal binary.
- Settings and profiles from 0.4.0 carry over automatically.
- Known macOS 27 Golden Gate issues: activating background windows may require a title-bar click, keyboard shortcut, or Option-click; Bluetooth cursor motion may be less smooth than USB.
