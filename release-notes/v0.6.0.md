MockTab 0.6.0 shows an interactive diagram for recognized tablets and pens.  It also reads more of the controls on older Cintiq, Intuos3, Graphire, and Bamboo tablets.

Contributions from @fAIseh00d (Intuos4 key displays and turned tablets), @CBBorah (Graphire 3), and @giacomodancona2-pixel (Cintiq 21UX2) shaped this release.

## Buttons pane

- An interactive tablet drawing shows where each key, ring, dial, and strip sits.
- Pen and mouse diagrams depict recognized models.

## Tablet orientation

- Changing a tablet's orientation carries key assignments to their new places.  Right-click the tablet diagram in the Buttons pane to adjust.
- Tablet Area orientation shortcuts: ⌘⌥1 through ⌘⌥4.
- Pen tilt turns with the tablet, so brushes lean the way the pen does.

## Improved tablets

- Cintiq 12WX, 20WSX, 21UX, 21UX2, and 22HD get touch strips and fuller ExpressKey support, with separate toggles and modes for each strip on the 21UX2 and 22HD.
- Barrel buttons on older Cintiqs release without delay.
- Every 8-key Intuos3 shows its touch strips.
- Graphire, Volito, and early Bamboo tablets switch into full pen mode at connect. Clicks register more reliably, the resting cursor holds still, and the Graphire 4 and Bamboo Fun mouse wheels read correctly.
- Bamboo and One by Wacom tablets, and the Intuos models built on them, register clicks more reliably and keep track of a pen hovering high.
- Intuos4 key labels wrap to fit and turn with the tablet. The key displays dim when idle and come back after the Mac sleeps, and the Intuos4 S and Intuos5 no longer offer displays they don't have.
- Intuos4 recovers when it slips back into mouse mode.
- The Intuos5 and Intuos Pro gen 1 wireless kit shows battery level, whether the tablet is linked or asleep, and a sleep timer in the Info pane.
- Intuos5 S (PTH-450) and Intuos Pro S (PTH-451) show their six ExpressKeys.
- Cintiq 24HD's right ring gets its own modes.
- Slow ring turns scroll smoothly instead of wobbling.
- Held ExpressKeys on Intuos Pro gen 3 over Bluetooth act as one press.
- Fingers no longer stick or vanish on Intuos5, Intuos Pro gen 1, and Bamboo Pen & Touch tablets.
- Wacom tablets MockTab doesn't know yet take their size from the tablet itself.

## Pen

- Art Pen 2 rotation reaches apps.
- The Intuos4 Grip Pen's side buttons and full pressure work on Intuos Pro (2017), over USB and Bluetooth.
- The cursor holds steadier on older Intuos tablets.
- A pen that leaves range over Bluetooth stays gone.

## Settings

- Settings follow Text Size, work from the keyboard, and read plainly with VoiceOver.
- Quick Keys settings edited in a tablet's Buttons pane follow the selected app.

## Reliability

- MockTab launches faster, and the pen works before settings windows open.
- No crash when a tablet is slow to answer at connect.
- An Intuos in Android mode gets a notice explaining how to switch it back.

## Compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later; universal binary.
- Settings and profiles from 0.5.2 carry over.
