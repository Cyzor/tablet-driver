MockTab 0.6.0 shows an interactive diagram for recognized tablets and pens, and reads more of the controls on older Cintiq, Intuos3, Graphire, and Bamboo tablets.

Contributions from @fAIseh00d (Intuos4 key displays and turned tablets), @CBBorah (Graphire 3), and @giacomodancona2-pixel (Cintiq 21UX2) shaped this release.

## Buttons pane and orientation

- A tablet drawing shows where each key, ring, dial, and strip sits; pen and mouse diagrams depict recognized models.
- Key assignments and pen tilt follow the tablet's orientation. ⌘⌥1 through ⌘⌥4 pick an orientation.

## Tablets

- Older Cintiqs (12WX, 20WSX, 21UX, 21UX2, 22HD) and every Intuos3 get touch strips and fuller ExpressKey support, with separate modes for each strip.
- Graphire, Volito, and early Bamboo tablets switch into full pen mode, click more reliably, and read their mouse wheels. Preliminary Graphire 3 Wireless support.
- Bamboo, One by Wacom, and related Intuos models click more reliably and track a high-hovering pen. Bamboo Touch ExpressKeys work.
- Intuos4 key labels wrap to fit, turn with the tablet, and dim when idle.
- Wireless kit battery level, link status, and sleep timer for Intuos5 and Intuos Pro gen 1.
- Cintiq 24HD's right ring gets its own modes, and slow ring turns scroll smoothly.
- Fingers no longer stick or vanish on Intuos5, Intuos Pro gen 1, and Bamboo Pen & Touch.

## Pen

- Art Pen 2 rotation, and Intuos4 Grip Pen side buttons and full pressure on Intuos Pro (2017).
- A steadier cursor on older Intuos tablets and Intuos Pro gen 3.
- Apps see each pen end as one tool, including the eraser over Bluetooth.
- Side buttons held near the edge of range stay held on Intuos4, Intuos5, and Intuos Pro gen 1.

## Settings and reliability

- Settings follow Text Size, work from the keyboard, and read plainly with VoiceOver.
- The Auto-Switch toggle leaves Profiles; per-app settings handle app-specific changes.
- Faster launch, with the pen working before settings windows open.
- No crash when a tablet is slow to answer at connect.

## Compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later; universal binary.
- Settings and profiles from 0.5.2 carry over.
