MockTab 0.5.2 brings support to more Cintiq pen displays. It also improves scrolling and touch capabilities for other tablets.

Reports and testing by @to-ma-s (multiple Cintiq models), @lcg-1111 (Cintiq 13HD Touch), @zsoltmar (Cintiq 27QHD Touch), @animaux (click properties), @ggy56hmcgv-a11y (CTL-6100WL, UI clarity), and @Autantpourmoi (multi-monitor configurations) shaped this release.

## Improved tablets

- Cintiq Pro 16 gets a working pen and ExpressKeys, including the ExpressKey Remote.
- Cintiq 13HD Touch gets preliminary pen and ExpressKey support.
- Cintiq touch, including the Cintiq 27QHD Touch, no longer blocks clicks.
- Cintiq brightness and contrast work over more connections.
- Intuos Pro gen 3 draws more evenly over Bluetooth.

## Pen

- Pen buttons can engage above the tablet's surface or only on contact.
- Side buttons can act as an eraser.

## Scrolling and touch

- Optional two-finger tap to right-click and three-finger drag.
- Improved scrolling with momentum enabled.
- Better palm rejection for touch-enabled tablets.

## Displays and mapping

- Span Displays button action toggles the pen's available range.
- More reliable reveal of a hidden Dock on multi-display arrangements.
- Calibration runs on the pen display itself, and its overlays show on any display.

## Compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later; universal binary.
- Settings and profiles from 0.5.1 carry over.
