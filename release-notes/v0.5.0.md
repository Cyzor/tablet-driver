MockTab 0.5.0 adds support for Intuos, Wacom One, Cintiq 24, and PL-series tablets, improves Intuos Pro gen 3 over USB and Bluetooth, and brings fuller pen input to Photoshop and iWork.

Reports and testing by @morinel (Wacom One S), @RenderWarrior (Bamboo Fun), @zsoltmar (Cintiq 27QHD Touch), @to-ma-s (Cintiq Pro 16), @Hickzor (screen mapping and modifier keys), @Autantpourmoi (multi-display mapping), and @elmimmo (pressure defaults on older Intuos tablets) shaped this release.

## New tablets

- Intuos S and M (CTL-4100WL, CTL-6100WL).
- Wacom One S and M (CTC-4110WL, CTC-6110WL), including Bluetooth.
- Wacom One 14 Touch, Cintiq 24, and Cintiq 24 Touch.
- PL-series pen displays.
- ExpressKey Remote, in preliminary form.

## Improved tablets

- Intuos Pro gen 3 (PTK-470, PTK-670, PTK-870) over USB and Bluetooth, with independent modes for each dial.
- Cintiq Pro and MobileStudio Pro touch.
- Touch on more Intuos5, Intuos Pro, and Bamboo models.
- Bamboo Fun and Cintiq 13HD Touch.
- Wireless dongles connect more reliably, and wireless input has less jitter.

## Pen

- Pages, Keynote, and Numbers get full pen input, including header and footer editing.
- Pressure uses the tablet's full resolution.
- Photoshop reads Art Pen barrel rotation directly, so the "Swap Tilt with Rotation" setting is gone.
- The eraser works on Intuos Pro over USB, and on Intuos Pro gen 3 over Bluetooth too.
- Held keyboard modifiers stay on pen drags.

## Scrolling and touch

- Pan View, touch scrolling, rings, strips, and dials scroll more smoothly.
- Touch follows the tablet's orientation and crop.
- Momentum scrolling is off for new installs. Some sites and apps, such as Calendar's Month view, only scroll with it off.

## Displays and mapping

- A new mapping view helps ensure a comfortable fit between tablets and monitors.
- A full-screen picker sets the screen area.
- You can span selected displays instead of all of them.
- Relative mode keeps the tablet's proportions.

## Diagnostics

- Help > Collect Device Data gathers everything in one zip, which you can send by email or through GitHub.
- MockTab > Check for Updates opens an update page.

## Compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later; universal binary.
- Settings and profiles from 0.4.2 carry over.
