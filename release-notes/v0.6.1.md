MockTab 0.6.1 fixes held shortcuts, taps, and pen clicks, supports the Cintiq 22HD's top keys, and adds preliminary Cintiq Partner support.

Contributions from @lantictac (ExpressKey holds), and reports and testing by @CBBorah (taps on small tablets), @43north (pen clicks on macOS 27.2), @artmisha-sketch (Cintiq 24HD Touch), @Fittkenny (pen responsiveness and Pan View direction), and others who helped privately shaped this release.

## Buttons and keys

- A held ExpressKey shortcut no longer stays down after the pen leaves range.
- Buttons can send F13 through F19, or open MockTab's settings.
- The Cintiq 22HD's three top keys work.
- A hand resting on a touch ring no longer scrolls.

## Pen

- Pen clicks and window activation should work on macOS 27.2.
- Wobbly taps stay clicks, and presses and window drags no longer stick on small tablets with large displays.
- An Art Pen on Intuos4 and Intuos5-era tablets updates twice as often.
- Pan View can reverse direction, and it and two-finger scrolling follow the Finder's column view more reliably.

## Tablets

- The Cintiq 24HD Touch pen works again alongside touch.
- The 2017 Cintiq Pro 16 should start its pen and touch reliably.
- Preliminary support for the Cintiq Partner (PTU-600), and for touch on the Bamboo Pen & Touch 6×8.
- Renaming a tablet works again.

## Settings

- Tablet Area shows the active area's size in millimeters or inches.
- The Profiles summary names each changed value.

## Compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later; universal binary.
- Settings and profiles from 0.6.0 carry over.
