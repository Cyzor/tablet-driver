MockTab 0.5.1 restores pen tilt and rotation in apps and fixes the Art Pen on older Intuos tablets.

## Fixes

- Apps see pen tilt again. MockTab 0.5.0 told apps that pens had no tilt, on every tablet, which could leave tilt unused or make rotation brushes unstable, as in Rebelle.
- Art Pen rotation reaches apps from the moment the pen comes into range, including after switching pens on an Intuos Pro gen 3.
- The Art Pen works on Intuos4, Intuos5, and Intuos Pro (PTH-x50, PTH-x51): it reports rotation and no longer clicks on its own.
- The eraser works while hovering on Intuos Pro (PTH-x60) over Bluetooth.
- Tilt reads at the right scale on the Intuos Pro S (PTH-460), Wacom One 12, and Wacom One Creative Pen Display.
- The Momentum Scrolling tooltips now say it's off by default.
