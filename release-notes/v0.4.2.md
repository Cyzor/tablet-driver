MockTab 0.4.2 adds a two-finger Rotate gesture, makes two-finger scrolling more reliable, and improves how pen and touch input hand off to each other.

Touch gesture work, including Rotate and concurrent pinch-and-rotate, are contributions from @MarsLuay. Thank you!

## Touch

- New two-finger **Rotate** gesture.
- Pinch and rotate can now run at the same time, and either can start after a gesture is already under way.
- Two-finger scrolling locks to the dominant axis, so a horizontal swipe scrolls Finder column view reliably instead of intermittently.
- Two-finger scrolls interrupted by a lifted finger now wind down cleanly instead of continuing on the remaining finger.
- Touch responsiveness setting: **Touch Onset Delay** (via `defaults`) tunes how quickly the first contact registers.
- Speed-dependent cursor gain for touch pointer motion.
- Disabled touch options are now shown greyed out rather than hidden.

## Pen and touch together

- Touch input resumes correctly once a busy pen goes idle, without waiting for a tip-down.
- Pen input is restored when a cable joins a live wireless session.
- Held pointer buttons are released on tool change and on full device disconnect.
- Bluetooth pen and touch report batches are paced together instead of arriving in bursts.

## Device support

- Touch now arms on the PTH-850 regardless of which USB interface enumerates first.
- Intuos Pro M touch support; Cintiq Pro 32 and other registry corrections.

## Fixes and reliability

- Stale hover-move events are suppressed under heavy report backlog.
- A stuck pen-proximity state is forced to exit when the tablet goes idle.

## Compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later; universal binary.
- Settings and profiles from 0.4.1 carry over automatically.
- Known macOS 27 Golden Gate issue: with momentum scrolling enabled, horizontal panning does not work in Finder column view. Turn momentum off for Pan View or touch two-finger scroll to restore it.
