MockTab 0.3.0 provides Xencelabs support, with a working driver for the Pen Display bundle — pen, Quick Keys, and the wireless dongle.  Also includes input‑reliability and interface improvements for all tablets.

0.2.6 shipped only as a pre‑release and included Wacom CTH‑690 support and an Accessibility permission prompt fix (see the [0.2.6 notes](v0.2.6.md)).

## Xencelabs

- **Xencelabs Pen Display Tablet support.** Pen input, Quick Keys puck, labels, and dial LED colors all function over USB and wireless relay.
- **Quick Keys dial modes can each have their own LED color**, set from color wells in the Buttons pane; the puck's light and screen stay in sync with the active mode.
- **A brightness slider for the Pen Display's built‑in panel** in the Displays pane — same effect as the bezel buttons, and MockTab restores your setting when the display reconnects.
- **Coverage note:** Support confirmed on Xencelabs Pen Display 24, including Quick Keys and its wireless dongle. The Pen Tablet Medium and Small share the same protocol and should also work. Xencelabs Pen Display 16 needs further verification.

## Input reliability

- **Pen input stays responsive while the system is under heavy load**. Background processing or heavy disk activity is less likely to induce laggy cursor movement.
- **Barrel buttons hold steady through brief sensing dropouts**, with better button release timing.
- **Easier activation of screen hot corners and auto‑hidden Dock.** MockTab now pins near‑edge positions exactly against the edge, like a mouse or trackpad.
- **Button fixes:** modifier keys sticking in some art apps, barrel buttons dropping during pen lift, a beachball when the tablet connects, the puck not recovering after a power cycle, and pen coordinates mapping to the wrong part of the screen.

## Interface

- The menu bar item is now a native status item with better item alignment and consistent behavior.
- Hold Option in the per‑app overrides list to clear every app's customizations at once.
- Touch ring and dial mode slots support a **Skip** setting, leaving them out of the mode‑button rotation entirely.
- Settings windows no longer snap to preset sizes after opening, stay resizable after switching tabs, no longer freeze their content during a resize, and restore their positions more reliably.
- The devices list shows details on a readable second line, with just the model number in the Kind column.

## Internal

- Display mapping gains its own component; window‑management classes renamed to match function.
- Revised source and contribution docs, including a new device‑support walkthrough in TabletKit.
- TabletKit decoder test suite: 322 tests, all passing.

## Compatibility

Same macOS requirement as 0.2.5: macOS 13 (Ventura) or later. Universal binary.

Settings and per‑app profiles carry over from all previous releases with nothing for you to migrate.
