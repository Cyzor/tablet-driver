MockTab 0.6.2 installs its own updates, lets buttons open apps, files, links, and Keyboard Maestro macros, and adds French and Italian.

Thanks to @juliusvaart for suggesting and testing updates, and to everyone who helped privately.

## New

- MockTab checks for, downloads, and installs updates from the Info pane or its menu bar item. It connects only to mocktab.org and sends no user data.
- Drag an app, file, link, or Keyboard Maestro macro onto a button's row, and the button opens or runs it.
- The Info pane shows live input as gauges.
- French and Italian, including macOS permission prompts.

## Tablets

- Preliminary support for the MobileStudio Pro's keys, rocker, and ring.
- A resting pen holds the pointer steady on older Intuos, Cintiq, Graphire, and Bamboo tablets.
- The Graphire4's pad wheel should scroll.
- Intuos4 key displays go dark when MockTab quits.

## Fixes

- Art Pen rotation works in Photoshop on its own, so its workaround setting is gone.
- Opening a second installed copy of MockTab brings the running one forward.
- Tablet diagrams draw cleanly on non-Retina displays.

## Compatibility

- Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later; universal binary. Settings and profiles from 0.6.1 carry over.
- 0.6.1 can't install updates: download this version once, and MockTab installs later versions itself.
